Betsson Group Responsible Gaming & Operating Policy
Version: 2026.1
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. Deposit Limits
Players can set daily, weekly, or monthly deposit limits through their account settings at any time. Decreases take effect immediately. Increases require a 24-hour cooling-off period before taking effect. Limits are applied per currency and per payment method. Once a limit is set, it cannot be removed, only adjusted. Players may request a permanent limit change through customer support with identity verification.

2. Self-Exclusion Protocol
A player requesting self-exclusion will have their account immediately locked for a minimum period of 6 months, up to permanent exclusion. Funds remaining in the balance will be refunded to the verified payment method within 5 business days. During self-exclusion, the player cannot open a new account, deposit, or place any bets. The exclusion applies across all Betsson Group brands. Players can request early removal only after the minimum 6-month period, and only after a mandatory responsible gaming review.

3. Bonus Wagering Terms
All promotional bonuses carry a standard 35x wagering requirement before bonus funds can be converted to real withdrawable cash. Wagering requirements must be met within 30 days of bonus activation. Different game categories contribute differently: slot games contribute 100%, table games contribute 10%, and live casino games contribute 5%. Bonuses are non-transferable and cannot be withdrawn directly.

4. Player Safety & Risk Escalation
If automated triggers detect signs of loss-chasing, rapid deposit frequency, or player distress, the account must be flagged for Responsible Gaming review and temporary cool-down. Automated triggers include: three or more deposits within one hour, deposit amount increasing by more than 200% within a session, or self-reported distress signals in support chats. Flagged accounts are reviewed by a trained Responsible Gaming specialist within 24 hours. The specialist may apply temporary limits, request a cooling-off period, or escalate to permanent self-exclusion based on the review.

5. Withdrawal Policy
Withdrawals are processed within 1-3 business days after identity verification. The minimum withdrawal amount is 10 EUR (or currency equivalent). Withdrawals must be returned to the same payment method used for deposits where possible. Large withdrawals (over 10,000 EUR) require additional source-of-funds verification as part of anti-money-laundering compliance.

6. Complaint Resolution
Players may file complaints through the in-app support system or by email. Complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 14 calendar days. Unresolved complaints may be escalated to the relevant licensing authority. All complaints and their resolutions are logged for regulatory audit.
